How it works

Loop: Chess move → capture triggers an RPG engagement → consequences return to the board.

Party rule: when piece A attacks piece B, every allied piece that directly threatens B joins the attacker's side; every enemy piece that directly threatens A joins the defender's side. Position pieces so your battles are won before they start.

Combat: attackers choose Quick ⚡ / Heavy 🗡 / Feint 🎭. Defenders time DODGE or BLOCK as a marker sweeps the bar — Perfect timing beats everything. Hits build Momentum → spend it on abilities. Winning combat captures the piece; losing means your attacker retreats wounded. You can also disengage (the defender escapes with a Fatigue debuff).

HP persists on the board. Wounded pieces fight weaker — heal slowly each turn. Damage to a King decides the match.
